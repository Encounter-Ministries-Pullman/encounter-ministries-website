Who we are

We are a diverse group of brothers and sisters united in our desire to experience God together,

so that the lost may encounter God through us.

Encounter Ministries is a local church family in Pullman, Washington. Whoever you are and wherever you are in your faith, there is a seat for you.

  • Non-denominational
  • Spirit-filled
  • Local church family
  • Pullman, WA

What we believe

Our faith rests on three foundations.

The Trinity

One God in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

The authority of Scripture

The Bible guides what we believe and how we live.

Salvation by grace

Received through faith, not earned.

Pastor Jordan Lachance

Pastor Jordan and Melanie Lachance are graduates of Christ for the Nations Institute in the Dallas-Fort Worth area, originally from the Northeast. They love people and carry a fire for the Holy Spirit, a heart for evangelism and pastoral ministry, and a passion to see people far from God planted firmly in the local church.
